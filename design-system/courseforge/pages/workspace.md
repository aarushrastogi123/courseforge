# Workspace Page Rules

The workspace is a focused lesson-authoring surface, not a marketing page or analytics dashboard.

## Layout

- Place the lesson workflow steps above the working area.
- Use a readable content width around 1100–1200px.
- On wide screens, place the primary brief form beside a compact guidance panel.
- On narrow screens, stack the guidance panel below the form.
- Keep the active step visually clear and label the other steps as upcoming.

## Content hierarchy

1. State the current step and why the instructor is here.
2. Collect the lesson title and one measurable learning objective.
3. Show the 45-minute target.
4. Explain the upcoming research and two review stages.
5. Offer one primary action to save or continue.

## Interaction

- Save the brief locally and make that storage behavior clear.
- Provide the HTTP Requests and Responses example as a secondary action.
- Place validation messages beside the field that needs attention.
- Announce save status without moving focus.
- Do not imply that research or generation has happened before those features exist.
